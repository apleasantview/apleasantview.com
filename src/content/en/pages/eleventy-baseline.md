## About Baseline

If you arrived from elsewhere and are wondering what this is: Baseline is a plugin for [Eleventy](https://www.11ty.dev/), a static site generator. It bundles the pieces a small business site usually needs (head metadata, multilingual handling, an internal content graph, a sitemap module) into one foundation, so you do not glue twelve plugins together.

This site is a working Baseline build. What you see here is what the plugin ships today.

For the project itself, including documentation and the full feature reference: [eleventy-baseline.dev](https://www.eleventy-baseline.dev/).

</section>
